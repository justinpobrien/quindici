How to play

Slide the tiles into order, 1 to 15, left to right from the top-left corner. The empty square ends up last.

Each color is a row: pink tiles belong in row one, yellow in row two, and so on. A tile turns green once it is home. Tap a tile in line with the empty square to slide it, along with any tiles between.

Daily and score

Everyone gets the same board each day. Par is the shortest possible solution. Your score is 1000 × precision × pace: precision drops as you go over par (each tile moved counts, and each hint adds 3), and pace drops slowly with time but never below half. Your first finished attempt is the one that counts.
