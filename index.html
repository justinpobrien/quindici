How to play

Slide the tiles into order, 1 to 15, left to right from the top-left corner. The empty square ends up last.

Each color is a row: pink tiles belong in row one, yellow in row two, and so on. A tile turns green once it is home. Tap or click a tile next to the empty square to slide it, or a tile further along the same row or column to slide them all.
