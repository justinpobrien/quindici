    How the score works

    score = 1000 × precision × pace
    precision = (par ÷ moves)²
    pace = ½ + ½ × 2−seconds ÷ (3 × par)

    Par is the true minimum. Every daily board is solved optimally ahead of time, so 1000 needs a perfect solve at instant speed.

    Moves matter most. Precision is squared. At 41% over par it has already halved your score.

    Time can only halve it. Pace loses half of its remaining bonus every 3 seconds per par move (2:24 today) and never falls below 50%.

    Hints cost 3 moves. Pausing hides the board.
